Most of us are quick to distance ourselves from Judas Iscariot. He betrayed Jesus. He was possessed by Satan. He died in disgrace.

He is not like us.

Or is he?

"What if I'm more like him than I thought?"

About the Book

Not to excuse him.
But to understand him.

In this carefully researched and deeply pastoral work, Dr. Benjamin Gilmore invites readers to look more closely at Judas than they ever have before. Not to excuse him — but to understand him. And in understanding him, to understand themselves.

"Judas was faithful before he fell. He was trusted before he betrayed. He was close to Jesus before he walked away."

Hello, My Name Is Judas asks the question most of us are afraid to answer: what drove this man? What were his hopes, his fears, his misplaced loyalties? And — most uncomfortably — where do we see ourselves in him?

This is not a book that lets Judas off the hook. It is a book that puts us on it — and finds, in that uncomfortable place, an invitation to honest, searching, transformative faith.

Includes a Small Group Discussion Guide.

About the Author

Benjamin Gilmore, DMin

Pastor · Professor · Author

Dr. Benjamin Gilmore serves as the founding pastor of North Gate Church in San Diego, California, and as an adjunct professor at The King's University, where he specializes in leadership.

He holds a Doctor of Ministry in Leadership from TKU and an MBA in Leadership and Entrepreneurship from UT Dallas. His previous books include Fear, No More: Fear God Evermore and The Night that Changed Hearts: A Christmas Story.

Hello, My Name Is Judas is his most ambitious work to date: years in the making, rooted in careful scholarship, and written for anyone who has ever read the Gospels and wondered about the man who walked closest to Jesus and then handed him over.
